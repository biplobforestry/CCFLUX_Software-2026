Sensitivity matrix

Every MIRO species against every recorded driver. %/unit is the change in the species, as a percentage of its own mean, per unit of the driver. Partial holds the other drivers fixed — a Zeppelin climbs as the day warms, so a simple slope against temperature also carries the vertical gradient. Where the two disagree by more than half, the simple slope is flagged confounded. Select a row to plot it.

Time series

Species and driver on a shared clock. A drift shows as the two moving together over hours; an atmospheric feature does not.

Species against driver

The fitted line is ordinary least squares. Its slope is the sensitivity quoted in the matrix.

Against the reference analyser

Only CO2, CH4 and H2O have a Picarro to compare against. The drift is fitted on the difference between the two analysers, not on the species itself — the species carries real atmospheric covariance with the driver, and removing that would delete signal rather than error.
